Hi,
I'm
Jomor

Montreal-based branding and web designer with a genuine passion for creating visual identities and websites that actually mean something.

I've been doing this since 2015 — working with startups, service businesses, restaurants, healthcare providers, and everyone in between. My approach is simple: understand the client, deliver something original, and make the process enjoyable for everyone involved.

I believe great design starts with a great relationship. That means asking the right questions, listening hard, and being honest about what will and won't work. No fluff. No generic templates. Just real, thoughtful work.

78% of my clients say I'm a genius. The other 22% say "sexy genius."

Sometimes I do great work. Other times I do mediocre push-ups. But I always show up and give it everything I have.
